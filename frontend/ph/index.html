Hybrid eligibility: built for sari-sari reality

1. Branded packaged goods (Lucky Me, Bear Brand, Pampers, sachets in box)

→ JAN/EAN-13 barcode scan from smartphone. Strict per-product eligibility.

2. Tingi / loose goods (rice by cup, sugar by kilo, single sachets)

→ Store MCC fallback. Sari-sari pre-approved as MCC 5411 (grocery) gets a monthly no-barcode budget cap (e.g. PHP 300 / month).

3. Liquor / tobacco / gambling (MCC 5921 / 5993 / 5813 / 7995)

→ Always blocked. Even if a "grocery" sari-sari diversifies into liquor, those merchants drop out of the whitelist.

Why Manila now?

  • 4Ps (Pantawid Pamilyang Pilipino Program) reaches 4.4M households / ~20M people — single-program scale that exceeds anything in Japan.
  • BSP regulatory sandbox already approved PHPC (2024) — the legal vehicle exists.
  • Typhoons hit ~20×/year + flooding + volcanic activity — CP-6 disaster fallback delivers value monthly, not once-a-decade.
  • GCash QR Ph is universally adopted at sari-sari level — payment rail is already there.
  • JICA's Digital Public Goods funding (¥30–100M/year) explicitly fits this model.
