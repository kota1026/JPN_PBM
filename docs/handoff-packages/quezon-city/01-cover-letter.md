# To: Honorable Mayor + Innovation Office, Quezon City

**Re**: Proposal for Quezon City as launch LGU — 4Ps Programmable Subsidy (PBM) pilot
**From**: JPN-PBM Project (Tokyo + Manila Twin Pilot)
**Date**: 2026-05-09 (draft)

---

Honorable Mayor and Innovation Office team,

We are an open-source international initiative led from Tokyo, designed to make municipal subsidy distribution **purpose-bound** through stablecoin technology. We have built a complete sandbox implementation (175+ tests, GitHub Actions CI green, 24-item smart-contract audit done at <https://github.com/kota1026/JPN_PBM>) and are now seeking a launch LGU for the **first 4Ps PBM pilot in the Philippines**.

**We propose Quezon City** as the host LGU, for these reasons:

1. **Quezon City is the largest 4Ps recipient ward in Metro Manila** by household count.
2. **DICT (Department of Information and Communications Technology)** has historical close working relations with QC, suitable for a digital pilot.
3. **Quezon City's Web3-receptive mayor** (we believe) makes the political path to approval shortest.
4. **Setagaya Ward (Tokyo) ↔ Quezon City sister-city** relationship since 1992 provides a natural diplomatic frame and PR angle.

What this pilot is — short version:

- 5 households × 5 sari-sari stores + 1 Mercury Drug + 1 7-Eleven, 3 months.
- 4Ps recipient scans QR Ph + product barcode on their own smartphone (no POS hardware needed at sari-sari).
- Subsidy paid in **PHPC** (BSP-approved peso stablecoin from Coins.ph).
- During typhoons, pre-signed offline QR coupons keep the program running offline (CP-6).
- All operations transparently logged on-chain.

What we ask from Quezon City:

| Step | What | Effort |
|------|------|--------|
| 1 | Designate a project liaison | 0.2 FTE for 6 months |
| 2 | Identify pilot merchants (5 sari-sari + 1 Mercury Drug + 1 7-Eleven) | 2 weeks of intro calls |
| 3 | Co-author merchant onboarding protocol | 1-day workshop |
| 4 | Provide an LGU office room for citizen onboarding | 1 morning |

**No direct LGU budget request** — costs covered by JICA Digital Public Goods grant + Coins.ph in-kind contribution.

What Quezon City gets:

- First-of-its-kind blockchain-based welfare pilot in the Philippines.
- OECD / BIS / MAS conference visibility as a Web3 leader.
- Strong sister-city moment with Setagaya / Tokyo.
- A working tool to reduce 4Ps leakage (estimated 5–8% currently to non-essential goods).

We would be honored by a 30-minute introductory meeting at the Mayor's or Innovation Office's convenience.

— JPN-PBM Project Lead
<https://github.com/kota1026/JPN_PBM>
