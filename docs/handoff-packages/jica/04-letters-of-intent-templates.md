# Letters of Intent — Templates (5 prepared)

> **Purpose**: Pre-drafted Letters of Intent (LoIs) for the 5 key partners listed in the JICA application v1 §4 (Counterpart Strategy). These are **starter drafts** — recipients will revise to fit their own legal language.
> **Status**: Round 15 (sandbox draft). Real signatures pending diplomacy.

---

## Why pre-draft these?

The JICA pre-screening team will ask "do you have any LoIs?" early in the conversation. Without templates, requesting signatures from 5 organizations sequentially can take **3–6 months**. With templates, it can take **2–6 weeks** (each partner edits their own copy).

Each template is intentionally:

- **One page** (recipients dislike multi-page LoIs they have to legal-review)
- **Non-binding** (LoI ≠ MoU; reduces approval friction)
- **Specific to one ask** (avoids "we'll think about it" ambiguity)
- **Signed by exactly two parties** (each LoI = bilateral)

## How to use these in the JICA conversation

| Situation | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| JICA asks "have you contacted any partners?" | "We have prepared 5 LoI templates (this document) and are ready to deploy them upon JICA introduction." |
| JICA asks for one signature first | Recommend Template 1 (JPYC) — least friction, internal company decision. |
| JICA asks for sign-off velocity | Each template ≤ 1 page, non-binding → ~2 week turnaround typical. |
| JICA wants to see real LoIs | Hand over LoIs that have been signed; re-share the templates for the rest. |
